<TextCol side="left">

A System Usability Scale score of 68 is the general usability benchmark. Zealty fell below it for both groups, but the gap differs by audience. Experts scored closer to the B2B professional-product benchmark of 57, meaning Zealty performs roughly in line with typical professional software. Novices scored well below that, showing the platform's complexity was a much steeper barrier for first-time buyers than for industry professionals.

</TextCol>

<TextCol side="right">

Both groups were tested on four tasks: finding an address, checking property details, navigating and sorting, and filtering. Filtering was the widest gap in the study - task 4 alone shows experts spending nearly three times longer on Zealty than on the competitor.

</TextCol>

On Task 4, complex filtering, experts made 4.6 times more errors on Zealty than on the competitor. The gap wasn't complexity itself - it was the interface breaking conventions professionals rely on every day.
