synopsis:
  - "I have always loved journaling. One of the main issues I've always had with journaling is knowing where to start, so I built myself a little AI companion that would help me do that. That's how Friendly came to be. Friendly is a companion that will have a friendly conversation with the user and then produce a well-written journal post. The user does not need to think about how to write or where to start. The companion will do that for them. Friendly is available through a monthly subscription based on how much the user wants to converse with the companion."
  - "The product is in its first phase. User analysis and research are ongoing to better understand users' needs and what new features to introduce next."
